WHERE IT FITS

If a customer would rather see it first, it belongs on video.

Seven categories where a live expert changes the sale and the service call. Pick one.

SELL

“Will it fit, and will it match?”

The expert measures the real piece on camera, shows the fabric in daylight, and the shopper flips their phone to show the room. Size and colour doubt disappears.

SUPPORT

A wobble after delivery, not a return

The customer shows the joint. The expert spots the misaligned panel, walks them through the fix, or sends the right part. No pickup, no replacement sofa.

SELL

Two models, side by side, real units

Capacity, drum, noise, the inside of the door. The expert runs both and the shopper picks the one that fits their family, not the one with the better photo.

SUPPORT

“It says E4 and won’t start”

The agent sees the display through the customer’s camera, reads the code, and resets or diagnoses it on the first call. If a part is needed, the technician arrives with it.

SELL

A private viewing, from the product page

The piece turns under proper light, goes on a hand or neckline, and sits next to two alternatives. Hallmark and weight on camera. Trust at a distance.

SUPPORT

Resize, repair, or verify before a visit

Clasp issues, resizing, certificate checks. The customer shows the piece and paperwork; the expert confirms what is needed before anyone travels to a store.

SELL

The walkaround, without the drive to the showroom

A live tour of the exact variant on the lot: boot space, rear legroom, the infotainment screen. Questions answered, test drive booked, on the same call.

SUPPORT

“A warning light came on”

The owner points the camera at the dashboard. The service advisor identifies it, checks if it is safe to drive, and books the right slot with parts ready. One visit instead of two.

SELL

Pick the right spec, not the loudest one

Screen, camera samples, ports, weight in hand. The expert compares two configurations live and the trade-in gets valued on camera. Fewer wrong buys, fewer returns.

SUPPORT

Setup and transfer, done together

SIM, data migration, accounts, connectivity. The agent sees the screen and walks through it step by step. The service centre trip disappears.

SELL

Router placement before the install

The shopper shows the home; the expert picks the plan, the router, and where it should sit. Fewer “no signal upstairs” calls after day one.

SUPPORT

“The connection keeps dropping”

The agent sees the router lights and the cabling, guides a reposition or reset, and confirms the fix on the recheck. The technician stays in the van.

SELL

Finish, hardware and fit, chosen on camera

Walnut or matte white, soft-close or push-to-open. The expert demonstrates real hardware, checks the measurements, and the quote goes out the same day.

SUPPORT

Installation help for dealers and homeowners

A drawer that doesn’t sit flush, a hinge that needs adjusting. The expert sees it and guides the fix, so the installer finishes on the first visit.
