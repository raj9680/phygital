Questions D2C teams ask first.

We still sell on marketplaces. Is this only for our webstore?

No. Use live demos to make your own store the better place to buy, and put a QR code in every box so marketplace buyers come to you for setup and support. Over time, more of the relationship moves to your channel.

We don't have people to run video calls. Who does?

Maxicus managed services can hire, train and run a dedicated team of video experts for your products. You can also use your own staff, or mix the two by product line and time of day.

Does it work with Shopify and our current site?

Yes. Phygitalmax adds a button and a video layer to the product pages you already have, on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or a custom build. Shoppers install nothing.

How do we know it's paying off?

Every session is tracked: conversion by channel, order value, cross-sell, repeat demos, and for support, field visits avoided and satisfaction scores.
